# 0121: Tools 1b, the sculpted ground, joins the 3D track

- **Status:** Accepted (the project lead, 2026-11-18)
- **Date:** 2026-11-18

## Problem

Decision 0108 brought Tools 1 (shape brushes and world sculpting) ahead of the remaining 2D. Tools 1a, the shape brushes, is built. Tools 1b, the sculpted ground, was next, and its first milestone (G1) was mostly built when the project lead looked at the whole roadmap on 18 November and asked to shelve the 3D world tools and put them where they fit in the 3D roadmap.

## Decision

- **Tools 1b moves into the 3D track,** after 3D 1 and before Open worlds. The ground, big worlds (Open worlds) and foliage come together as one stretch of 3D world work.
- **G1's work is kept** on the branch `tools-1b/g1-ground`, unmerged, and picks up from there.
- The ground study, and decisions 0111, 0116 and 0117, stand as they are.

## Consequences

- ROADMAP.md's Tools 1b section moves after 3D 1, and Open worlds follows it.
- The README tracker and CLAUDE.md say where Tools 1b waits.
- The 2D track and the shared phases come sooner. What comes next is the project lead's to say.
