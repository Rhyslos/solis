# editor/src/brushing

Making shape brushes (Tools 1a, B2; [decision 0111](../../../../docs/decisions/0111-tools-1-brushes-and-ground.md); the approved mock-ups [`01-brush-list.png`, `02-brush-draw.png` and `03-brush-edit-faces.png`](../../../../docs/design/tools-1/README.md)): the brush list **B** opens, drawing the shape taken out on the ground, and (B3) editing a brush's insides: **Tab**, then **1**, **2**, **3** for points, edges and faces. `../brushing.rs` holds `Brushing` (the list and the drawing, kept by the viewport), what the status bar and the Hints card say, each shape's icon, and the counts a drawn-out shape starts with (`settings_for`).

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `list.rs` | `List`, the brush list while open: the shape highlighted (B moves it down, wrapping), where it floats beside the pointer; `show` draws it over everything ("SHAPE BRUSHES", a row a shape with its icon, "B next · Space use · Esc close") and says whether a row was clicked or a click landed elsewhere. |
| `draw.rs` | `Draw`, a brush being drawn out, as arithmetic: `update` takes each frame's mouse (`DrawInput`) and what the view shows (`DrawView`) and steps from waiting, to the footprint dragged (snapped to the grid unless Ctrl is held), to the height pulled up (`Lift`: up a line standing on the footprint's middle, or up the screen when the camera looks straight down), to a `Planned` brush on a click; a footprint or height of nothing makes nothing and says why (`Nothing`), and a right-click under way gives up. `Plane` is where the footprint lies: on the ground or on top of what's under the pointer, or a flat view's own plane (2D, Front, Right: one grid step deep, no height). The Ramp and Stairs are turned to climb the longer side from where the drag started (`climb`). Tests in `draw_tests.rs`. |
| `ghost.rs` | What's drawn over the view meanwhile: the footprint and its corners, the shape's ghost (its few edges and faces, by the painter, never through the area's brushes), each size in metres beside its edge, the height's arrow, the steps at the top of the view and the keys at its foot. |
| `edit.rs` | `Editing`, a brush whose insides are edited: its mode (`Mode`: points, edges, faces; switching keeps the pick as the new mode's parts), what's picked and under the pointer (`Part`, by the mesh's ids, so a pick survives moves, undo and redo), the undo state it began at, its picking geometry and a drag under way; the status bar's and the view's words; `points_of` (the points picked parts move) and `counts`. |
| `parts.rs` | `PartCache`, finding a brush's parts, made once per mesh: the face under the pointer (the brush's box first, then each face's box, then its triangles; only the brush being edited), its nearest corner or side on screen in points or edges mode, and every part whose place is inside a box (through the brush). Tests in `parts_tests.rs`. |
| `reshape.rs` | `Drag`, a gizmo drag of picked parts: each frame's change (snapped by the caller) made again on the brush as it was, in its own space; let go, checked by `mesh::check_moved` (only the faces touching what moved) and given back, or refused with why. `stretched` stretches a brush about its middle to a size typed on its card. Tests in `reshape_tests.rs`. |
| `edit_draw.rs` | What's drawn over the view while editing: the brush's edges facing the camera, a dot on each point or at each face's middle, the picked parts in the accent and the one under the pointer lit, the text at the top ("Editing Box_03 · Faces · 1 picked") and the keys at the foot. |
| `card.rs` | The Brush card in Properties: Made as, Size (typing it stretches the brush), Material (unfinished), Solid with its note, the three count boxes (the mode's lit while editing), and "Picked: 1 face · Faces mode". |
| `surface.rs` | What a drag starts on: the first brush triangle (or model box) the pointer's ray meets, found once as the button goes down; each brush's box first, only the boxes hit tried triangle by triangle, nearest first. |
| `tests.rs` | The counts a drawn-out shape starts with, the icons, and what the status bar and Hints say. |
| `picture_tests.rs` | The list open, a box being drawn out, and a box's insides edited in faces and in points mode, drawn for real in the sample project and held to their approved pictures (`crates/editor/tests/pictures/brush-list.png`, `brush-draw.png`, `brush-edit-faces.png`, `brush-edit-points.png`). |

The workbench's side (B, Space and Esc; the list drawn at the frame's end; the brush added as one undo step, named and picked; Made as making a brush again) is `../workbench_brushes.rs`, tested as a person uses it in `../workbench_brushes_tests.rs`; the Shape button is a line of `../shell/ribbons/home.rs`; the viewport hands the mouse over in `../viewport_view.rs` (`draw_out`, and `find_parts` while editing). Editing's workbench side is `../workbench_brush_edit.rs` and `../workbench_brush_parts.rs`, its ribbon `../shell/ribbons/brush.rs` (HOME TOOLS · Brush).
